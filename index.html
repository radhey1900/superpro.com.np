Why It Hurts — We Understand

Your laptop broke. How did it feel?

We know. That sinking feeling when your screen cracks, your battery dies, or your laptop just won't turn on. Your work, your studies, your livelihood — everything stops. First you cry. Then you look for help. We are here.

😱

Broken / Cracked Screen

One drop, one press, one accident — and your screen is shattered. You can't see anything. You can't work. You can't study. We replace it with OEM-grade panels — True Tone and color calibration restored too.

😤

Battery Dies Too Fast

You plug in every hour. Your laptop won't last you even one meeting, one class, one trip. You carry the charger everywhere. We replace with 100% health cells — no system warnings, full life back again.

💧

Water / Liquid Spilled on It

Your chai, your coffee, your water bottle — spilled on your laptop by accident. Panic. You think it's dead. 90%+ of these we bring back to life — ultrasonic cleaning, micro-soldering, component-level rescue.

😰

Won't Turn On — Dead Motherboard

You press the power button. Nothing. No light. No sound. You try charging. Still nothing. You feel your stomach drop. We diagnose at the motherboard level — find the short, fix the IC, bring it back.

😨

BGA / Chip-Level Failure

Your laptop suddenly shuts down, overheats, shows blue screen, or the GPU/CPU fails. This is the hardest repair — most shops turn you away. Not us. We do BGA reballing, chip replacement, micro-soldering — permanently.

😞

Slow / Old / Can't Keep Up

Your laptop was fast once. Now it freezes, hangs, takes forever to start. You're thinking of buying a new one. Wait — we upgrade your SSD, add RAM (up to 32GB), migrate your data. Faster than new, half the cost.
